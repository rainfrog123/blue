for giffgaff, you have to top up then you can use it ?

Gemini said

Yes, you do need to top up or buy a plan to use a giffgaff SIM card. Giffgaff SIM cards arrive blank and do not have a phone number attached to them until they are activated.

To activate your SIM and get your number, you must make an initial purchase. You have two options for this:

  • Add Pay As You Go Credit: The minimum amount you can top up is £10.

  • Buy a Monthly Plan (Goodybag): You can directly purchase a one-month plan, which starts at a minimum of £6.

You can make this initial payment using a credit/debit card, a PayPal account, or a top-up voucher bought from a store. Once you make the payment and the SIM is fully activated, your new giffgaff number will be assigned to you and you can start using your phone.
